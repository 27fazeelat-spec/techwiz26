How it works

From policy PDF to a plan you can defend

Four stages. Only one of them uses generative AI, and it never has the final word.

  1. 01

    Read

    PDF and Word policies are parsed with page and paragraph references. Hidden or injected instructions are quarantined before anything else sees them.

    Python
  2. 02

    Ground

    Every "must" and "shall" becomes a requirement with its source. Contradictions are resolved by your precedence rules. A person approves the role matrix.

    Python + people
  3. 03

    Generate

    Gemini drafts modules, checklists, tasks, quizzes and assessments for one employee, citing a document and section for every item.

    Gemini
  4. 04

    Verify & approve

    {{ m.rules }} deterministic rules check coverage, sources, facts, stages and contradictions. Anything doubtful goes to a reviewer before assignment.

    Python + people
