Stage02

Build the ground truth, and let a person approve it

Every "must", "shall" and "required" becomes a requirement with its obligation, due stage and source. When two documents disagree, your precedence rules pick the winner and explain why. The resulting Role Requirement Matrix is approved by a person before any plan is judged against it.

  • {{ m.recall }}% recall and {{ m.precision }}% precision against a {{ m.requirements }}-clause answer key
  • Numbers, deadlines, frequencies and permission vs prohibition compared across documents
  • Equal authority? The conflict waits for a reviewer, and the decision is kept

Stage03

Gemini drafts a plan for one person

For a named employee, Gemini chooses which clauses apply and drafts modules, objectives, checklists, tasks, scenarios, quizzes and rubric-based assessments. Output is structured JSON, validated against a schema, and every item must cite a document and section.

  • Versioned prompt templates; model, prompt version and source versions logged for every call
  • Bounded retry, JSON repair and a fallback model when the service is slow or over quota
  • Sources are delimited as data; overridden and quarantined clauses are never sent

Stage04

Python checks every line, independently

{{ m.rules }} deterministic rules compare the draft with the approved matrix and the source text. None of them imports or asks a language model. The result is a score you can recompute and a status you can explain.

  • Mandatory coverage, source validity, role and condition fit, stage and sequence
  • Numeric facts and quiz answers must appear in the cited clause
  • GenAI vs Python comparison, field by field, for every requirement

Stage05

A person decides what Python could not

Unsupported items and missing requirements go to a review queue. Reviewers approve, reject, edit, override with a reason, or regenerate a single module. A plan can only be assigned when nothing is left waiting.

  • Edits are schema-checked and the whole plan is re-validated
  • Regeneration touches one module; decisions on unchanged items carry over
  • Every decision is written to an append-only audit trail

Stage06

Learn, measure, and keep up with policy changes

New hires work through checklists, tasks signed off by their manager and quizzes scored against the verified answer key. When a policy changes, the platform shows what changed, which items and people it affects, and rebuilds only those modules.

  • On Track, Requires Attention, Behind Schedule, Assessment Required, Completed
  • Weak areas from wrong answers; recommendations a manager accepts or dismisses
  • Twelve reports exported as CSV, Excel and PDF
