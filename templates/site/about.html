About

Why we built a pipeline that checks its own AI.

SkillSprint AI is our entry to the TechWiz Generative AI PowerPlay competition (theme: OnboardVerse). The brief asked for personalised onboarding from company documents. We decided the interesting problem was not generating it, but proving it right.

The idea

A new hire should never learn a rule that isn't in the policy, or miss one that is.

Language models are excellent at turning dense policy into readable lessons, and just as good at inventing a plausible deadline that appears nowhere in the source.

So we split the work. Deterministic Python builds the ground truth from the documents, a person approves it, Gemini drafts against it, Python checks the draft line by line, and a person signs off. Every number on this site comes from that pipeline running on its own test data.
