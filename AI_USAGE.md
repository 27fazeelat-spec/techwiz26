# AI Usage Declaration

AI-assisted tools were used during the development of SkillSprint for limited support activities. AI assistance was used in preparing and structuring the project dataset, including the creation and organization of the 185+ requirement records and supporting sample documents. AI assistance was also used during development when specific UI, backend, debugging, or implementation issues required additional guidance.

The core system design, integration, validation logic, database implementation, testing, and final decisions were reviewed and controlled by the project team. Any AI-assisted output was reviewed, modified where required, and tested by the team before being incorporated into the project.

The Gemini API used within SkillSprint is part of the application's functionality, rather than a development tool.
