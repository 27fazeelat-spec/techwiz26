## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope

This policy covers every Saffron Table kitchen, dining room and takeaway counter.

## 2. Training

### 2.1 Food Safety Level 1

All employees must complete Food Safety Level 1 training within the first week.

### 2.2 Refresher

Shift supervisors must repeat food safety refresher training every six months.

## 3. Temperature control

### 3.1 Chilled food

Chilled food must be stored at or below 5 °C.

## 4. Allergens

### 4.1 Menu information

Allergen information must be available for every dish on the menu.

### 4.2 No guessing

Employees must never guess whether a dish contains an allergen; they must check the allergen chart or ask the chef.

## 5. Illness

### 5.1 Stomach illness

Employees with vomiting or diarrhoea must not handle food until 48 hours after their symptoms have stopped.
