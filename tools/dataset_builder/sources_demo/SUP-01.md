## 1. Deliveries

### 1.1 Delivery windows

Deliveries arrive between 7:00 and 9:00 on weekdays. The driver waits while chilled goods are checked.

### 1.2 Checking chilled goods

Chilled deliveries must be checked with a probe thermometer and rejected if they are above 5 °C.

Questions about an order go to the Freshfield customer desk.
