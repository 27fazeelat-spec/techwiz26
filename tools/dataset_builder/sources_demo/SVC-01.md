## 1. Welcoming guests

### 1.1 Greeting

Servers must greet every table within 2 minutes of the guests sitting down.

### 1.2 Allergies first

Servers must ask every table about food allergies before taking the order.

## 2. Serving food

### 2.1 Temperature at the pass

Hot food must be held at or above 65 °C until it is served.

### 2.2 Allergy dishes

Dishes for a guest with an allergy must be carried to the table separately and named when they are served.

## 3. When something goes wrong

### 3.1 Complaints

Complaints must be reported to the shift supervisor before the end of the shift and written in the complaint log.

## 4. Payments

### 4.1 The float

Servers must count their cash float at the start and end of each shift and sign the float sheet.

### 4.2 Card payments

Payment cards must stay in the guest's sight at all times.
