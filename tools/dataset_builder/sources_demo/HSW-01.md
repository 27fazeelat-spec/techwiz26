## 1. Injuries

### 1.1 Reporting

Every injury, however small, must be reported to the shift supervisor straight away and written in the accident book.

### 1.2 Burns and cuts

Kitchen staff must know where the first aid kit and burns kit are kept by the end of the first day.

## 2. Fire

### 2.1 Before the first shift

All employees must know the fire exits, the extinguishers and the assembly point before their first shift.

### 2.2 Fire drill

Shift supervisors must run a fire drill with their team every six months.

## 3. Working safely

### 3.1 Knives

Knives must be carried with the blade pointing down and never left in the washing-up sink.

### 3.2 Footwear

Kitchen staff must wear closed, non-slip safety footwear.
