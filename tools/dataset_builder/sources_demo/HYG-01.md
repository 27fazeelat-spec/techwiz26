## 1. Handwashing

### 1.1 Before handling food

Kitchen staff must wash their hands for at least 20 seconds before handling food and after touching raw meat.

### 1.2 Hair and jewellery

Kitchen staff must wear a hair covering and remove rings and watches before starting a shift.

## 2. Storage and temperature

### 2.1 Chilled storage

Chilled food must be stored at or below 4 °C, with fridge temperatures checked and logged twice per shift.

### 2.2 Hot holding

Hot food must be held at or above 63 °C until it is served.

### 2.3 Labels

Line cooks must label every prepared item with the date it was made and a use-by date.

## 3. Cleaning

### 3.1 Chopping boards

Kitchen porters must wash and sanitise chopping boards after each use.

### 3.2 Chemicals

Cleaning chemicals must be stored in the chemical cupboard, away from food, and never decanted into unlabelled bottles.

### 3.3 Closing clean

Kitchen porters must complete the closing cleaning checklist before the end of every shift, from the first day.
