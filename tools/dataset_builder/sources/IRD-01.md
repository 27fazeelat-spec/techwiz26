## 1. Scope and training

### 1.1 Training

Employees assigned to In-Room Dining must complete In-Room Dining Service training before their first room-service shift.

### 1.2 Service overview

In-Room Dining delivers meals and refreshments to guest rooms and serviced apartments. Orders arrive by phone, through the guest app or from the TV menu.

## 2. Taking and delivering orders

### 2.1 Operating hours

Room-service orders may only be accepted during the operating hours listed for the property in Annex A; orders outside these hours must be referred to the Duty Manager.

### 2.2 Order taking

The order taker repeats the order back to the guest, asks about allergies and quotes an estimated delivery time.

### 2.3 Delivery times

Standard delivery time is 30 minutes for breakfast and 35 minutes for all-day dining.

### 2.4 Guest verification

Before delivery, the associate must confirm the guest's name against the room number in the PMS and must not announce the room number aloud in public areas.

## 3. Collection

### 3.1 Tray pick-up

Guests place used trays outside the room or call the order line for collection.

### 3.2 Corridor collection

Trays and trolleys placed in corridors must be collected within 60 minutes of the guest placing them outside the room.

## 4. Assessment

### 4.1 Entry procedure

In-Room Dining associates must demonstrate the knock, announce and wait-for-invitation entry procedure during a role-play assessment.
