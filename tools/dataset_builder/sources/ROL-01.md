## 1. Front Office Associate

### 1.1 Purpose of the role

The Front Office Associate is the guest's first and last point of contact, handling arrivals, departures, payments and guest requests at the front desk.

### 1.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Front Office Manager. Key competencies:

- Warm, confident guest communication
- Accurate PMS operation and cash handling
- Discretion with guest personal data

### 1.3 Service standard

Front Office Associates must complete a standard check-in in under 5 minutes once certified.

### 1.4 Upselling

Front Office Associates are responsible for offering room upgrades using only the rates available in the PMS.

## 2. Guest Relations Executive

### 2.1 Purpose of the role

The Guest Relations Executive builds relationships with VIP, returning and long-stay guests and leads service recovery when things go wrong.

### 2.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Guest Relations Manager. Key competencies:

- Empathy and calm under pressure
- Complaint resolution using the CARE model
- Excellent spoken English plus one additional language

### 2.3 VIP contact

Guest Relations Executives must contact every VIP and returning guest within 2 hours of arrival.

### 2.4 Incident log review

Guest Relations Executives must review the Guest Incident Log at the start of every shift.

## 3. Housekeeping Supervisor

### 3.1 Purpose of the role

The Housekeeping Supervisor leads a team of room attendants and guarantees that every room released to Front Office meets the Aurelle standard.

### 3.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Executive Housekeeper. Key competencies:

- Attention to detail
- Team leadership and scheduling
- Safe use of cleaning chemicals

### 3.3 Lost key cards

Housekeeping Supervisors may open guest rooms for guests who have lost their key card after checking the guest's name.

### 3.4 Team briefing

Housekeeping Supervisors must allocate room attendant sections and brief the team before every shift.

## 4. F&B Associate

### 4.1 Purpose of the role

The F&B Associate serves guests in restaurants, cafés, banqueting and, where assigned, In-Room Dining.

### 4.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Outlet Manager. Key competencies:

- Menu and allergen knowledge
- Food safety and hygiene
- Friendly, attentive table service

### 4.3 Menu knowledge

F&B Associates must know the daily menu, specials and allergen information before service begins.

### 4.4 Service Excellence certificate

F&B Associates should achieve the Aurelle Service Excellence certificate within 60 days.
