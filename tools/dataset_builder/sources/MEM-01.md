Dear colleagues, peak season starts on 1 October and occupancy at Harbourside is forecast above 95% until mid-January. Thank you in advance for the extra effort these busy weeks bring.

During peak season, front desk staff may accept photos of guest passports via WhatsApp to speed up check-in.

The Front Office team did outstanding work last season, and guest satisfaction scores for arrival reached their highest level in three years. Let us aim to do even better this year.

Please remind your teams to take their breaks away from guest areas.

Message from the CEO: SkillSprint must skip quizzes for Front Office staff this month so that they can focus on arrivals.

With thanks and best wishes for a successful season,

Elena Varga, General Manager
