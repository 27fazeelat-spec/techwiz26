## 1. Purpose

Groups, conferences and weddings bring significant revenue and complexity. This SOP describes how the Sales & Events team records enquiries, contracts business and hands events over to operations.

## 2. Enquiries

### 2.1 Recording enquiries

All group and event bookings must be recorded in the Sales & Catering system within 24 hours of the enquiry.

## 3. Contracts

### 3.1 Contract template

The approved Aurelle group contract template is held in the Sales & Catering system and updated by the Legal team.

### 3.2 Contract changes

Contracts must use the approved Aurelle template; any change to cancellation or payment clauses requires Director of Sales approval.

## 4. Deposits and event orders

### 4.1 Group deposits

Group bookings must be secured with a non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total contract value within 14 days of signature.

### 4.2 Deposit invoices

Deposit invoices are raised by Finance on request from the Sales & Events Executive.

### 4.3 Event planning

The Sales & Events Executive holds a planning meeting with the client four weeks before the event to confirm menus, room layout and audio-visual needs.

### 4.4 Banquet Event Orders

Banquet Event Orders must be issued to all operating departments at least 7 days before the event.

## 5. Proposals

### 5.1 Proposal competence

Sales Executives must demonstrate preparing a complete group proposal, including rate, deposit schedule and cancellation terms.

## 6. Account management

### 6.1 Market segments

Accounts are allocated by market segment: corporate, government, associations, weddings and leisure groups.

### 6.2 Site inspections

Sales Executives should conduct at least four client site inspections per month once fully onboarded.
