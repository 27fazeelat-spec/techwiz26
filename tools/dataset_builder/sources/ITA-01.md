## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how access to Aurelle systems is requested and granted, and how systems and devices are used day to day.

## 2. Access

### 2.1 Requesting access

System access is requested by the line manager through the IT portal and is granted only after the Information Security Basics module has been completed.

### 2.2 Standard access pack

The standard pack includes email, the HR portal and the learning platform. Role-specific systems such as the PMS or ERP are added according to the role.

### 2.3 Access levels

PMS access levels are assigned by role; associates must not request or use a higher access level than their role requires.

## 3. Credentials and devices

### 3.1 Passwords

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long.

### 3.2 Password resets

Password resets are available through the self-service portal or the IT Service Desk.

### 3.3 Staff Wi-Fi

Staff Wi-Fi is separate from the guest network and covers all back-of-house areas.

### 3.4 Personal devices

Personal devices must not be connected to the staff Wi-Fi without enrolment in the Mobile Device Management app.

## 4. Acceptable use

### 4.1 Business use

Company email and systems must not be used for personal commercial activities.

## 5. Mobile learning

### 5.1 The Aurelle Learning app

The Aurelle Learning app gives access to onboarding modules and quizzes from a phone.

### 5.2 App access

Employees may request access to the Aurelle Learning app on their personal phone after MDM enrolment.
