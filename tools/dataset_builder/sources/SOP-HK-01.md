## 1. Purpose

A clean, safe and well-prepared room is the core of the Aurelle stay. This SOP describes the cleaning standard, the supervisor inspection and the procedure for rooms displaying Do Not Disturb.

## 2. Room standard

### 2.1 Cleaning and release

Check-out rooms must be cleaned to the 42-point standard and inspected by a supervisor before being released as Vacant Clean in the PMS.

### 2.2 The 42-point standard

The 42-point standard covers the bathroom, bed, surfaces, refreshment tray, amenities, windows, lighting and technology. Each point appears on the room attendant's (housekeeper's) checklist in the inspection app.

### 2.3 Stay-over rooms

Stay-over rooms receive a daily service between 09:00 and 17:00 and a turndown service in the evening on request.

### 2.4 Supervisor inspections

Supervisors must inspect a minimum of 12 rooms per shift using the room inspection app and record all defects.

## 3. Do Not Disturb

### 3.1 DND rooms

Rooms displaying Do Not Disturb must not be entered; if the sign remains after 15:00, the supervisor must inform the Duty Manager for a welfare check.

### 3.2 Contacting the guest

Before a welfare check, Front Office attempts to reach the guest by phone.

### 3.3 Welfare checks

A welfare check must be performed by two employees, one of whom is required to be the Duty Manager or a Security officer.

## 4. Defects

### 4.1 Common defects

Common defects include dripping taps, blown bulbs, stained carpets and faulty air conditioning.

### 4.2 Logging defects

Engineering defects found during inspection must be logged in the maintenance system within 30 minutes.

## 5. Team management

### 5.1 Floor rotation

Supervisors should rotate room attendants between floors monthly to balance workload.
