## 1. Purpose

The night audit closes the hotel's business day. It confirms that every room, rate and payment recorded during the day is complete and correct before the PMS rolls over to the next date.

## 2. Timing and reconciliation

### 2.1 Audit window

The night audit must start at 02:00 and be completed before 05:00.

### 2.2 Before the audit

Before 02:00, the night team completes late arrivals, posts outstanding outlet charges and confirms that all cashiers have closed their shifts.

### 2.3 Cashier reconciliation

Before running the day-end, the auditor must reconcile all cashier Shift Close Forms against the PMS cash report.

## 3. Audit checks

### 3.1 Room charges

Occupied rooms without a posted room charge must be investigated and reported to the Duty Manager before day-end.

### 3.2 No-shows

Guaranteed reservations that did not arrive are posted as no-shows according to the reservation guarantee type.

### 3.3 Room status

The auditor compares the housekeeping room status report with the PMS and lists any discrepancies for the morning team.

### 3.4 Rate exceptions

Rate overrides above 15% of the published rate without an approval code must be listed on the Rate Exception Report for Revenue review.

## 4. Reports

### 4.1 Day-end

Running the day-end rolls the PMS date forward and produces the standard night audit reports.

### 4.2 Night audit pack

The night audit pack, containing the reports listed in Annex 1, must be saved to the Finance shared folder before 06:00.

## 5. Finance review

### 5.1 Clearing exceptions

Finance must review the night audit pack and clear all exceptions within one working day.
