## 1. Training

### 1.1 Scope

This SOP covers every Aurelle kitchen, restaurant, café, banqueting area and in-room dining service.

### 1.2 Food Safety Level 1

F&B employees must complete Food Safety Level 1 training before handling open food.

## 2. Temperature control

### 2.1 Chilled storage

Chilled food must be stored at or below 4 °C, with temperatures checked and logged twice per shift.

### 2.2 Probe thermometers

Each kitchen holds calibrated probe thermometers. Engineering checks calibration monthly and records the result on the thermometer tag.

### 2.3 Hot holding

Hot food held for service must be kept at or above 65 °C and discarded after 2 hours.

## 3. Allergens

### 3.1 Declared allergies

When a guest declares a food allergy, the server must check the Allergen Matrix for every dish ordered and inform the chef in person.

### 3.2 Allergy orders

Allergy-declared orders must be marked with a purple allergy flag on the kitchen ticket and served by the same server who took the order.

### 3.3 The Allergen Matrix

The Allergen Matrix lists every dish on each outlet menu against the fourteen major allergens, including peanuts, tree nuts, milk, egg, fish, shellfish, wheat, soy and sesame. The head chef updates it whenever a recipe changes.

## 4. Personal hygiene

### 4.1 Illness

Food handlers must report symptoms of vomiting or diarrhoea and must not work with food until 48 hours after symptoms stop.

### 4.2 Handwashing

Dedicated handwash basins with soap and paper towels are located at every kitchen entrance.

## 5. Development

### 5.1 Beverage service course

Associates should complete the Coffee & Tea Service Fundamentals course within 60 days.
