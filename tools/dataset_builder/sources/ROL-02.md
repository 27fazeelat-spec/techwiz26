## 1. Maintenance Technician

### 1.1 Purpose of the role

The Maintenance Technician keeps guest rooms, public areas and plant equipment in safe working order through planned maintenance and fast response to guest requests.

### 1.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Chief Engineer. Key competencies:

- Electrical, plumbing and HVAC fundamentals
- Safe isolation and permit discipline
- Courteous behaviour in guest areas

### 1.3 Minor repairs

Maintenance Technicians may carry out minor electrical repairs in occupied guest rooms if the guest agrees.

### 1.4 Preventive maintenance

Maintenance Technicians must complete the planned preventive maintenance tasks assigned in the maintenance system each week.

## 2. Duty Manager

### 2.1 Purpose of the role

The Duty Manager leads the property during the shift, resolving escalations, supporting every department and representing the General Manager to guests.

### 2.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Hotel Manager. Key competencies:

- Decision-making under pressure
- Incident and crisis management
- Broad knowledge of all hotel departments

### 2.3 Authority

The Duty Manager is the senior manager on duty outside office hours and has authority over all operational decisions until relieved.

### 2.4 Property walk-through

Duty Managers must complete a property walk-through, including back-of-house areas, at least twice per shift and log it.

## 3. Sales & Events Executive

### 3.1 Purpose of the role

The Sales & Events Executive wins and manages group, conference and event business for the property.

### 3.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Director of Sales. Key competencies:

- Negotiation and proposal writing
- Understanding of rate strategy and displacement
- Event planning and client care

### 3.3 Accounts and targets

Sales & Events Executives are responsible for accounts in their assigned market segment and for meeting the quarterly revenue target set by the Director of Sales.

### 3.4 Event handover

Sales & Events Executives must hand over each confirmed event to the Events Operations team at least 14 days before the event.
