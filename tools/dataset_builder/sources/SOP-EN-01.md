## 1. Purpose and training

### 1.1 Purpose

Engineering work keeps every Aurelle property running, but electrical, mechanical and hot work carry serious risk to technicians, colleagues and guests. This SOP describes the permit and isolation controls that manage that risk.

### 1.2 Training

Technicians must complete Work Permit and Lockout/Tagout training before carrying out any electrical or mechanical work.

## 2. Permit to Work

### 2.1 Permit types

Electrical work, hot work and work at height require a signed Permit to Work issued by the Chief Engineer or Duty Engineer.

### 2.2 Permit contents

Each permit records the location, the task, the isolation points, the PPE in use, the permit validity period and the names of everyone working under it.

### 2.3 Guest rooms

Electrical work in guest rooms may only be carried out when the room is vacant; if the room is occupied, the guest must be moved or the work rescheduled.

## 3. Lockout/Tagout

### 3.1 Isolation

Before starting work, the technician must isolate the energy source, apply a personal lock and tag, and test for zero energy.

### 3.2 Lock issue

Each technician receives personal red locks and tags engraved with their name, issued by the Chief Engineer.

## 4. Hot work

### 4.1 Definition

Hot work is any activity that produces flames, sparks or heat capable of starting a fire.

### 4.2 Hot work controls

Hot work such as welding, cutting or grinding may only be performed by technicians holding a valid hot work certificate, with a fire watch present for 30 minutes after completion.

## 5. Guest requests

### 5.1 Response times

Guest-room maintenance requests must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and resolved, or the guest updated, within 2 hours.
