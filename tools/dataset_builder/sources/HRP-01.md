## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes the core employment terms that apply to every Aurelle employee, at every property and at the corporate office. Country-specific employment contracts sit alongside this policy.

## 2. Probation

### 2.1 Probation period

New employees must complete a six-month probation period, with formal reviews at the end of months one, three and five.

### 2.2 Purpose of reviews

Probation reviews give the new employee and the manager a structured moment to discuss progress against the onboarding plan, strengths and areas for development. Review outcomes are recorded in the HR portal.

### 2.3 Thirty-day check-in

Employees must complete a 30-day check-in meeting with their reporting manager and record it in the HR portal.

## 3. Working time

### 3.1 Standard hours

Standard working time is 48 hours per week across six shifts; overtime is required to be approved in advance by the department head.

### 3.2 Rotas

Department rotas are published two weeks in advance on the staff notice board and in the HR portal. Shift swaps between colleagues go through the department supervisor.

### 3.3 Rest days

Each employee receives one rest day per week, scheduled on the rota.

### 3.4 Night shifts

Employees working night shifts must take a paid 30-minute break between 02:00 and 04:00 and must not leave the property during it.

## 4. Attendance

### 4.1 Punctuality

Shifts start at the time on the rota, in full uniform, at the employee's workstation.

### 4.2 Absence reporting

Absence due to illness must be reported to the supervisor at least two hours before the start of the shift.

## 5. Grievances

### 5.1 Raising a grievance

Grievances must be raised first with the direct supervisor and, if unresolved within five working days, escalated in writing to Human Resources.

### 5.2 Confidentiality

Grievances are handled confidentially. HR records the outcome and any agreed actions in the employee's personnel file.

## 6. Career development

### 6.1 Internal vacancies

Internal vacancies are advertised on the HR portal before external recruitment begins.

### 6.2 Internal transfers

Employees may apply for an internal transfer after completing 12 months of service.
