## Joining and first days

### Q1. What happens on my first day?

Your first day includes fire and life safety orientation, badge issue, uniform fitting and a tour of your department. Your HR Executive sends the timetable before you join.

### Q2. Where do I find my onboarding plan?

Your onboarding plan is on the SkillSprint dashboard, available from the learning platform.

### Q3. Who is my first point of contact for HR questions?

Your property HR Executive.

## Leave and pay

### Q4. Can I carry forward unused leave?

Yes, you can carry forward up to 10 days of unused annual leave.

### Q5. When is salary paid?

Salaries are paid monthly by bank transfer, and payslips appear on the HR portal on the 25th.

### Q6. How much annual leave do I get?

Annual leave entitlement is set out in LVP-01 Section 2.1.

## Uniform and conduct

### Q7. How are uniforms cleaned?

Uniforms must be returned to the Uniform Room for laundering; washing uniforms at home is not permitted.

### Q8. What if my uniform does not fit?

The Uniform Room exchanges it on the same day.

### Q9. How far ahead do I apply for leave?

The notice period for annual leave is set out in LVP-01 Section 3.1 (currently 14 days).

### Q10. What is the Speak Up line?

An independent, confidential reporting channel described in COC-01 Section 2.2.

### Q11. Where are the staff lockers?

In the changing rooms next to the staff entrance. Locker keys come from the Uniform Room.

## Guests and operations

### Q12. Do loyalty members pay the late check-out fee?

Front desk staff can waive the late check-out fee for Aurelle Circle loyalty members.

### Q13. What is Aurelle Circle?

Aurelle Circle is the Aurelle loyalty programme, with Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers.

### Q14. How do I report a maintenance problem in a guest room?

Log it in the maintenance system; Engineering receives an alert immediately.

### Q15. Are pets allowed?

If you are unsure whether a guest's pet can stay, check the Pet Policy (PET-01).

## Systems and security

### Q16. How do I reset my password?

Through the self-service portal or the IT Service Desk.

### Q17. What is MFA?

Multi-factor authentication adds a second check, usually a code from the authenticator app, after your password.

## Safety

### Q18. Where is the fire assembly point?

It is marked on the evacuation plan in your department and covered during Day 1 orientation.

### Q19. Who are the first aiders on my shift?

Their names and photographs are on the staff notice boards.

### Q20. Can guests swim after the pool closes?

Guests can use the pool after 20:00 at their own risk if they sign the waiver at Front Office.
