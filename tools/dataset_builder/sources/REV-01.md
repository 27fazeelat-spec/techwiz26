## 1. Purpose

Room revenue is Aurelle's largest income stream. This policy protects rate integrity across every channel and sets out who decides on overrides, waivers and group pricing.

## 2. Rate integrity

### 2.1 Loaded rates only

Only rates loaded in the PMS by Revenue Management may be offered to guests; manual rate creation is prohibited.

### 2.2 Channels

Rates are distributed through the Aurelle website, the reservations centre, online travel agencies and corporate agreements. Parity between channels is monitored daily.

### 2.3 Aurelle Circle rates

Aurelle Circle loyalty members see member rates when logged in to the website or the app.

### 2.4 Rate overrides

Rate overrides above 15% of the published rate require an approval code from the Duty Manager or a Revenue Analyst.

## 3. Fees and waivers

### 3.1 Fees in scope

This section covers early check-in, late check-out, cancellation and no-show fees.

### 3.2 Published fees

Fee amounts are published in the Front Office SOP (SOP-FO-01) and on the Aurelle website.

### 3.3 Waiver records

Every waiver appears on the daily Rate Exception Report reviewed by Revenue Management.

### 3.4 Late check-out waivers

Late check-out fees may be waived only by the Duty Manager, and the reason for the waiver must be recorded in the PMS.

## 4. Reporting

### 4.1 Daily reporting

Revenue Analysts must produce the daily pick-up and pace report by 10:00 each day.

### 4.2 Report audience

The pick-up and pace report goes to the General Manager, Director of Sales and department heads.

### 4.3 Forecast review

Forecast accuracy must be reviewed weekly, and variances above 5% must be explained at the weekly revenue meeting.

## 5. Group pricing

### 5.1 Displacement analysis

Group quotes above 10 rooms per night must be evaluated with the displacement calculator before being offered.
