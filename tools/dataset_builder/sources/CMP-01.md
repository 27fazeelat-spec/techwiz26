## 1. Purpose

This document sets the group-wide calendar for mandatory onboarding training and the rules for mandatory assessments. It takes precedence over local training schedules.

## 2. Mandatory training calendar

### 2.1 Fire and Life Safety

Fire and Life Safety orientation must be completed on Day 1.

### 2.2 Guest Data Privacy

Guest Data Privacy training must be completed within Week 1.

### 2.3 Information Security acknowledgement

All employees must acknowledge the Information Security Policy in the HR portal within their first week.

### 2.4 Calendar overview

| Training | Audience | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Fire and Life Safety orientation | All employees | Day 1 |
| Information Security Basics | All employees | Week 1 |
| Guest Data Privacy | Roles handling guest data | Week 1 |
| Payment Card Handling | Roles processing payments | Week 1 |
| Food Safety Level 1 | Food & Beverage | Week 1 |

## 3. Assessment rules

### 3.1 Pass mark

All mandatory e-learning modules in the onboarding plan must be completed with a pass mark of at least 80% within 30 days of joining.

### 3.2 Attempts

Each mandatory assessment allows up to three attempts. Results from every attempt are kept in the learning record.

### 3.3 Coaching review

Employees who fail a mandatory assessment twice must be referred to their manager for a coaching review before a third attempt.

## 4. Delegation of authority

### 4.1 Approval limits

Employees with approval authority must sign the Delegation of Authority acknowledgement before exercising any approval limit.
