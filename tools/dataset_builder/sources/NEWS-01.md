## Recipe of the month: chef Anand's mango lassi

Blend two ripe mangoes, 400 g of plain yoghurt, 200 ml of cold milk, two teaspoons of sugar and a pinch of cardamom until smooth. Serve chilled with a sprinkle of pistachios.
