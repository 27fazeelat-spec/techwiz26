## 1. Purpose

This policy explains annual leave, sick leave and other leave types at Aurelle Hotels & Residences. It applies to all employees at all properties and at the corporate office.

## 2. Annual leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 30 calendar days of annual leave per year, accrued monthly from the date of joining.

### 2.2 Accrual

Leave accrues on the first day of each month. The HR portal shows the current balance, leave already taken and approved future leave.

### 2.3 Public holidays

Public holidays follow the official calendar of each country. Employees who work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off, scheduled by the department.

### 2.4 Carry-forward

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next year; any excess days lapse on 31 March.

## 3. Requesting leave

### 3.1 Notice

Annual leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave.

### 3.2 Approval

The department head reviews requests in the order received, taking into account occupancy forecasts and team cover.

### 3.3 Blackout dates

Annual leave will not be approved during property blackout dates published by the General Manager, except in documented emergencies.

## 4. Sick leave

### 4.1 Paid sick leave

Paid sick leave follows the employee's country contract.

### 4.2 Medical certificates

Sick leave of more than two consecutive days must be supported by a medical certificate submitted within 48 hours of return.

## 5. Other leave

| Leave type | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Marriage leave | 5 working days | Once during employment |
| Compassionate leave | 3 working days | Immediate family |
| Maternity leave | As per country contract | Paid in line with local terms |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days | Within 3 months of the birth |
