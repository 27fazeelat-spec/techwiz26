## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Why this code exists

Guests choose Aurelle because they trust us. This code describes the behaviour that protects that trust, between colleagues and with every guest, supplier and partner.

### 1.2 Scope

The code covers all employees, including trainees and temporary staff, at every property and at the corporate office.

### 1.3 Declaration

All employees must sign the Code of Conduct declaration before their first shift.

## 2. Respect at work

### 2.1 Harassment and discrimination

Harassment, discrimination or bullying of guests or colleagues is prohibited and must be reported through the Speak Up line or to Human Resources.

### 2.2 The Speak Up line

The Speak Up line is an independent, confidential reporting channel available by phone and web, 24 hours a day, in English, Arabic and Malay. Reports are reviewed by the Group Ethics Officer.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality

### 3.1 Background

Guests sometimes offer small gifts as thanks for good service. Aurelle welcomes this appreciation while keeping decisions free from influence.

### 3.2 Gift limit

Employees may accept unsolicited gifts from guests up to a value of USD 25; any gift above this value must be declared to the Duty Manager within 24 hours.

## 4. Appearance

### 4.1 Uniform

Employees must wear the full Aurelle uniform and name badge while on duty, following the grooming standard in Annex A.

### 4.2 Uniform issue

Uniforms are issued by the Uniform Room on Day 1 and laundered by the property.

### 4.3 Name badges

Name badges show the employee's first name and the languages they speak.

### 4.4 Maintenance workwear

Maintenance Technicians are exempt from the uniform standard in Section 4.1 and must instead wear the engineering workwear and PPE required by HSP-01.

## 5. Social media

### 5.1 Guest privacy online

Employees must not post photographs of guests, guest rooms or back-of-house areas on personal social media.

### 5.2 Official channels

Aurelle's official social media accounts are managed by the Communications team at the corporate office.

## 6. Integrity

### 6.1 Honest records

Accurate records, from time sheets to guest folios, are the foundation of trust with guests and auditors.

### 6.2 Company property

Company equipment, systems and supplies exist for Aurelle business.

### 6.3 Conflicts of interest

Conflicts of interest, including family relationships with suppliers, must be declared to Human Resources in writing.

## Annex A: Grooming Standard

| Element | Aurelle standard |
|---|---|
| Hair | Neat and tidy; long hair tied back in food and housekeeping areas |
| Nails | Short and clean; neutral colours only |
| Jewellery | One ring and one pair of small earrings |
| Footwear | Black, closed, polished shoes |
| Fragrance | Light and subtle |
