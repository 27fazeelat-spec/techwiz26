## 1. Purpose

These procedures keep Aurelle's spending controlled and its records audit-ready. They cover purchasing, supplier payments, credit to corporate clients, expense claims and group deposits.

## 2. Purchasing

### 2.1 Purchase Orders

Purchases above USD 1,000 require an approved Purchase Order before the order is placed with the supplier.

### 2.2 Approved suppliers

Aurelle's approved supplier list is maintained by the Purchasing Manager and published in the ERP.

### 2.3 Goods receipt

The receiving store records a goods received note in the ERP when a delivery arrives.

### 2.4 Three-way match

Supplier invoices must be matched to the Purchase Order and the goods received note before payment.

## 3. Credit

### 3.1 City ledger credit

City ledger credit above USD 5,000 per account must be approved by the Finance Manager before the account is activated.

## 4. Expenses

### 4.1 Expense system

Expense claims are submitted through the expense module of the ERP.

### 4.2 Claim deadline

Expense claims must be submitted with original receipts within 30 days of the expense.

## 5. Group deposits

### 5.1 Deposit invoicing

Finance issues deposit invoices for group and event bookings on request from Sales & Events.

### 5.2 Deposit level

Group deposits of 25% of the contract value must be received before a booking is confirmed as definite.

## 6. Month-end

### 6.1 Supervised close

Finance Associates must complete a supervised month-end accounts payable close before working independently.
