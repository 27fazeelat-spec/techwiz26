## 1. Revenue Analyst

### 1.1 Purpose of the role

The Revenue Analyst turns booking data, market intelligence and forecasts into pricing recommendations that maximise room revenue.

### 1.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Director of Revenue. Key competencies:

- Advanced spreadsheet and reporting skills
- Forecasting and pricing analysis
- Clear communication of numbers to non-specialists

### 1.3 Rate setting support

Revenue Analysts are responsible for supporting the Director of Revenue in setting daily rates across all distribution channels.

### 1.4 Competitor rate shop

Revenue Analysts must maintain the competitor rate shop and update it daily by 09:00.

## 2. Finance Associate

### 2.1 Purpose of the role

The Finance Associate keeps the property's accounts accurate and on time, from supplier payments to corporate client credit.

### 2.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the Finance Manager. Key competencies:

- Accounts payable and receivable
- Reconciliation and attention to detail
- ERP finance module operation

### 2.3 Transactions and approvals

Finance Associates process accounts payable and city ledger transactions and must follow FIN-01 for all approvals.

### 2.4 ERP training

Finance Associates must complete ERP Finance module training before posting any journal entry.

## 3. HR Executive

### 3.1 Purpose of the role

The HR Executive supports the full employee journey at property level, from recruitment and onboarding to employee relations and exits.

### 3.2 Reporting line and competencies

Reports to the HR Manager. Key competencies:

- Employment administration
- Confidentiality and data protection
- Coaching managers on HR processes

### 3.3 Employee relations

HR Executives are the first point of contact for employee relations queries at property level.

### 3.4 HR System training

HR Executives must complete HR System administrator training before creating personnel files.
