## 1. Purpose and terms

### 1.1 Terms used

In this document, Manager on Duty (MOD) means the Duty Manager on shift at the property. Outside office hours the MOD is the most senior manager present.

## 2. Reporting incidents

### 2.1 Injuries

Any injury to a guest or employee must be reported to the Manager on Duty (MOD) within 15 minutes.

### 2.2 How to reach the MOD

The MOD carries the duty phone at all times. The number is printed on the back of every staff ID badge.

### 2.3 Data breaches

Suspected data breaches must be reported to the Manager on Duty and the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.

## 3. Management escalation

### 3.1 Severity 1 incidents

The Duty Manager must notify the General Manager within 30 minutes of any Severity 1 incident, meaning death, serious injury, fire, police involvement or a data breach.

### 3.2 Incident reports

The Duty Manager must complete the Incident Report in the incident system before the end of the shift for all Severity 1 and 2 incidents.

### 3.3 Severity levels

| Severity | Examples | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Death, serious injury, fire, police involvement, data breach | MOD, General Manager, Group Security |
| 2 | Minor injury, theft, guest dispute, property damage | MOD, Department Head |
| 3 | Service failure, minor complaint | Department supervisor |

## 4. Communications

### 4.1 Media enquiries

Employees must not speak to the media about any incident and must refer all enquiries to the Communications team.

## 5. Training

### 5.1 Crisis simulations

The corporate Security team runs a crisis simulation at each property once a year.

### 5.2 Crisis Communication training

Duty Managers must complete Crisis Communication training within their first two weeks.
