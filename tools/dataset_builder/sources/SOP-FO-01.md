## 1. Purpose and terms

### 1.1 Purpose

Arrival and departure are the moments guests remember most. This SOP describes how Front Office welcomes, identifies and settles guests at every Aurelle property.

### 1.2 Terms used

The guest folio (also called the guest bill) is the running record of all charges and payments for a stay. The PMS is the Property Management System.

## 2. Check-in

### 2.1 Identity verification

At check-in, the associate must verify the guest's identity against a valid passport or national ID and scan it into the PMS.

### 2.2 Welcome

The associate greets the guest by name where known, confirms the length of stay, rate and room type, and explains breakfast times and Wi-Fi access.

### 2.3 Standard check-in time

Standard check-in time is 15:00 at all properties.

### 2.4 Early arrivals

Early check-in up to 2 hours before the standard 15:00 check-in time is free of charge, subject to availability; earlier arrivals are charged 50% of the nightly rate.

## 3. Check-out

### 3.1 Check-out time

Standard check-out time is 12:00; late check-out is charged at 50% of the nightly rate until 18:00 and a full night thereafter.

### 3.2 Express check-out

Guests who have settled their guest bill in advance receive a final invoice by email and simply leave their key card in the express box.

### 3.3 Fee waivers

Late check-out fees may only be waived in line with REV-01 Section 3.4.

## 4. Settlement

### 4.1 Payment methods

Accepted payment methods are listed in the Payment Card & Cash Handling Policy (PCH-01).

### 4.2 Folio settlement

Associates must settle the guest folio, obtain the guest's agreement to all charges and close the payment on the terminal before issuing the final invoice.

## 5. Room keys

### 5.1 Key cards

Key cards are encoded at the front desk for the length of the stay and expire automatically at check-out time.

### 5.2 Replacement keys

Replacement room keys may only be issued at Front Office after verifying the guest's identity against the PMS record; other departments must not open rooms for guests.

## 6. Certification

### 6.1 PMS certification

Associates must complete the PMS Front Desk certification before working a shift alone.

### 6.2 Certification format

Certification consists of a half-day classroom module followed by a practical test on the training PMS.

### 6.3 Experienced associates

Associates with two or more years of previous front office experience may take the PMS certification test directly without attending the classroom module.

## 7. Residences properties

### 7.1 Long-stay guests

At Residences properties, long-stay guests of 30 nights or more must re-verify their identity document every 90 days.
