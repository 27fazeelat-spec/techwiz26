## 1. Purpose

This policy covers everyday workplace safety at Aurelle: reporting injuries, personal protective equipment (PPE), manual handling and the safe operation of pools and leisure areas.

## 2. Injuries

### 2.1 Reporting injuries

Workplace injuries, however minor, must be reported to the supervisor and recorded in the incident log before the end of the shift in which they occur.

### 2.2 Why minor injuries matter

Small cuts, slips and strains reveal hazards before they cause serious harm. The Safety Committee reviews the incident log every month.

## 3. Personal protective equipment

### 3.1 PPE for chemicals and tools

Safety footwear, gloves and eye protection must be worn when handling cleaning chemicals or using power tools.

### 3.2 PPE issue

PPE is issued free of charge by the Housekeeping and Engineering stores and replaced when worn.

### 3.3 Chemical handling training

Employees who handle cleaning or pool chemicals must complete Chemical Handling training before first use.

## 4. Manual handling

### 4.1 Heavy loads

Loads heavier than 20 kg must be lifted by two people or moved with a trolley.

## 5. Pools and leisure areas

### 5.1 Pool facilities

Eight Aurelle properties have outdoor pools, and three have indoor leisure pools with a gym.

### 5.2 Pool hours

The pool must be closed to guests at 20:00 and at any time a lifeguard is not on duty.

### 5.3 Signage

Pool rules, depth markings and opening hours are displayed at every pool entrance.

### 5.4 Lifeguard certificate

Employees assigned to pool duty must hold a valid lifeguard certificate before their first pool shift.

### 5.5 Water testing

Pool water chlorine and pH levels must be tested and logged every 4 hours during opening hours.
