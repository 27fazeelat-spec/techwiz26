## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes how property HR teams prepare for a new hire, create and protect personnel records, and manage leavers.

## 2. Before the first day

### 2.1 Right-to-work checks

Right-to-work documents must be verified and copied to the personnel file before the employee's first working day.

### 2.2 Offer pack

The offer pack contains the contract, the job description, the joining instructions and a link to the pre-joining portal.

### 2.3 Personnel files

Personnel files must be created in the HR system within 2 working days of joining; paper files are not permitted.

## 3. Day 1 preparation

### 3.1 Scheduling Day 1

HR Executives must schedule the new hire's Day 1 orientation, badge issue and uniform fitting before the joining date.

## 4. Confidentiality

### 4.1 Sensitive records

Salary, disciplinary and medical records are the most sensitive information held by HR.

### 4.2 Sharing with managers

Salary and disciplinary information must not be shared with line managers beyond the minimum necessary for approved HR processes.

## 5. Competence

### 5.1 Onboarding file practical

HR Executives must demonstrate processing a complete onboarding file, including contract, right-to-work check and probation schedule.

## 6. Leavers

### 6.1 Exit interviews

Exit interviews must be offered to every leaver and recorded within 5 working days of the last working day.
