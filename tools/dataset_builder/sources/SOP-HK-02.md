## 1. Purpose

Guests leave belongings behind every day. A clear, recorded process protects the guest's property and protects colleagues from misunderstandings.

## 2. Logging found items

### 2.1 The register

All found items must be logged in the Lost & Found register within 1 hour of being found.

### 2.2 Register details

Each entry records the date, time, location, a description of the item, the finder's name and a unique reference number.

### 2.3 Valuables

Valuables, including cash, jewellery, electronics and documents, must be sealed in a tamper-evident bag and handed to Security in line with the Security SOP.

## 3. Storage and return

### 3.1 Retention periods

Non-valuable items must be kept for 90 days and valuables for 180 days before disposal or donation.

### 3.2 Storage room

The Lost & Found store is a locked room in the Housekeeping office, with access limited to supervisors.

### 3.3 Guest enquiries

Guest enquiries come in by phone, email or at the front desk, and Front Office searches the register by date and description.

### 3.4 Returning items

Items may only be returned after the owner provides a description matching the register and shows photo ID.

## 4. Disposal

### 4.1 Donation

Properties may donate unclaimed non-valuable items to an approved charity.
