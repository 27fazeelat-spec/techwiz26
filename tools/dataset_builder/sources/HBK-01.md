## 1. Welcome to Aurelle

### 1.1 About this handbook

Welcome to Aurelle Hotels & Residences. Since opening our first hotel in Dubai in 2009, Aurelle has grown to twelve hotels and serviced-apartment properties across the United Arab Emirates, Oman and Malaysia. Around 2,400 colleagues look after our guests every day, from the front desk to the engineering workshop.

This handbook is a practical introduction to working at Aurelle. It summarises the most important points of our policies and procedures in plain language. The full policies are listed in Section 8, and where this handbook and a policy differ, the policy is the authoritative source.

### 1.2 Your acknowledgement

All employees must read the Employee Handbook and sign the acknowledgement form on their first working day.

The acknowledgement form is part of your Day 1 pack, and your HR Executive collects it during orientation.

## 2. Your first days

### 2.1 Identification

Employees must collect their staff ID badge from Human Resources on Day 1 and wear it visibly at all times while on property.

### 2.2 Getting around

Every property has a staff entrance, a staff canteen and changing rooms with lockers. Your supervisor gives you a short tour of back-of-house areas on your first shift, including the uniform room, the time office and the staff notice boards.

### 2.3 Your onboarding plan

Your onboarding plan sets out the modules, checklists, practical tasks and assessments for your role, arranged in stages from Day 1 to your first 90 days. Your plan is personal: it reflects your role, your department, your property and your previous experience.

### 2.4 Attendance

Employees must use the staff entrance and record attendance on the biometric clock at the start and end of every shift.

## 3. Learning and development

### 3.1 The Aurelle Academy

The Aurelle Academy offers e-learning, classroom sessions and on-the-job coaching. Most mandatory training is delivered through the learning platform, and your progress is visible to you and your manager.

### 3.2 Culture path

New employees are encouraged to complete the Aurelle Culture e-learning path within their first 30 days.

## 4. Pay and benefits

### 4.1 Salary and payslips

Salaries are paid monthly by bank transfer. Payslips appear on the HR portal on the 25th of each month.

### 4.2 Benefits

Benefits vary by country and grade and include medical cover, duty meals, uniform laundering and discounted stays at Aurelle properties through the colleague rate programme.

## 5. Health and hygiene at a glance

### 5.1 Personal hygiene

Clean uniforms, tidy grooming and regular handwashing are part of the guest experience at Aurelle. Full grooming standards are in the Code of Workplace Conduct.

### 5.2 Food areas

Colleagues working in kitchens and restaurants follow the Food Safety & Allergen SOP. The summary below gives the key figure new colleagues ask about most often.

### 5.3 Food storage summary

Chilled food must be kept at or below 5 °C.

## 6. Safety

### 6.1 Fire drills

Employees must take part in the annual fire drill held at their property.

### 6.2 First aid

Every property has trained first aiders on each shift. Their names and photographs are displayed on the staff notice boards and next to each first aid box.

## 7. Conduct

### 7.1 Our values

Aurelle's values are Warmth, Integrity, Precision and Pride. They guide how we treat guests, colleagues and partners.

### 7.2 Gifts

Employees must not accept gifts of any value from guests or suppliers.
